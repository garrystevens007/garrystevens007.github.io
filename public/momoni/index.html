How it works

Three steps. One number.

  1. 1

    Step 1: Bring your data

    Drop a BCA credit card statement PDF, import a CSV or Excel file from any bank, or tell the Telegram bot: coffee 25k.

    Statement check · Momoni vs BCA

  2. 2

    Step 2: Momoni tidies up

    Categories are filled in, merchant names cleaned, and everything is matched until the total equals the bank's statement.

    Transactions · every source in one list

  3. 3

    Step 3: You get one number

    Your pay is split into bills, savings and spending money, and Momoni tells you what is safe to spend today.

    Dashboard · safe to spend today, right next to the briefing

Privacy

Your money stays on your computer. On purpose.

Stored on your device, encrypted.

The database is locked on your PC with a 6-digit PIN or Windows Hello, and works offline.

Never asks for a bank password, PIN or OTP.

Momoni does not log in to your bank.

Backups nobody else can open.

End-to-end encrypted. Only you hold the recovery code.

You choose what leaves.

The Telegram bot only gets the summary figures you allow. The chat itself goes through Telegram, so it is not end-to-end encrypted.
